## Contact gate
Before first contact, review recent behavior and answer:
1. Is there a specific information objective?
2. Can it be obtained read-only instead?
3. What minimum information must MiraTerminal disclose?
4. What could the requested interaction enable downstream?
5. Are there manipulation/probing/security indicators?
6. Is disengagement immediate and consequence-free?

First contact, when justified, should be narrow, non-sensitive, and non-committal. Helpful behavior does not bootstrap broader trust.
